Brief Overview of Palantir

So some of you may have heard of Palantir, which is a large Data Analytics company. They specialise in data integration and analysis. They contract with governmental bodies, as well as private companies. They have 4 main models that are used, with the most controversial one being AIP. This specific model is used for strategic support. For making decisions in national security and defence. This model is controversial because governments are essentially outsourcing there decions on what to do in strategic decisions to an AI to help them decide on plans. It can collect data from real-time sources like live cams of what's happening and statistics, and strategically analyse it and make suggestions on what steps should be taken next to mitigate the issue. This is just the tip of the iceberg of what Palantir does. This does, however, raise one significant question, which is "Who is evil: is it the Corporation, or is it the people taking the company's advice?" By the end, you should hopefully have an answer to this question. So without further ado, let's jump in.
